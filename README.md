# DJ Radio

DJ Radio is a 24/7 radio station that runs on your own Windows PC, from the music folder you already have. You control it from a dashboard in your browser. OBS loads a player page for the stream. Fans can get a link where they vote on the next hour's genre, or ask for a song.

Your music folder is only read. Nothing in it is moved, renamed, or copied. Votes and settings stay on your PC.

**DJ Radio needs a key to run.** Try it free for **12 hours**: type your email, no card. The demo key belongs to AI Playground LLC; you borrow it for 12 hours, it is not your key or an account, and each email gets one demo. To keep the station running, subscribe for **$5/month** and paste your own key. When a demo or subscription ends, the station stops and asks for a key; your settings are kept.

## Install

1. Download `DJ-Radio-v2j.zip` and unzip the `DJ Radio` folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click **Start DJ Radio.cmd**. A black window opens and your browser opens DJ Radio. **Leave this window open.**
3. Choose **Start 12-hour free demo** (email only), **Subscribe $5/month**, or **I have a key** (paste the key shown after you pay).
4. Follow the setup screens. The last one gives you the OBS link and the vote link.

During the demo, the top of the dashboard shows the time left, with a **Subscribe** button next to it.

After setup, double-clicking **Start DJ Radio.cmd** opens the dashboard again.

## Stop the station

Click **Stop DJ Radio** on the dashboard, or double-click **Stop DJ Radio.cmd** in your DJ Radio folder, then close any leftover black window. You can also close the Start window. Stopping also stops OBS/player audio. The **Stop DJ Radio** button is also on the key screen, so you can always stop the station, even after a demo or subscription ends.
